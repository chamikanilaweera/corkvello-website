THE CEREMONY

How to use CORKVELLO™

From sealed foil to aerated glass in four effortless steps.

1

Cleanly slice the seal

Place the ergonomic foil cutter over the bottle lip and twist once to remove the capsule cleanly, revealing a pristine cork surface.

2

Extract with a single touch

Hold CORKVELLO vertically over the bottle neck. Press and hold the lower extraction button; the precision spiral will gently penetrate and lift the cork in seconds.

3

Release the cork smoothly

Press the upper release button to automatically eject the cork from the transparent chamber, fully intact and ready to inspect.

4

Aerate, pour, and preserve

Insert the aerating pour spout to decant directly into your glass, or seal with the vacuum stopper to keep remaining wine fresh for your next gathering.

Follow the included owner's guidebook for detailed care and maintenance guidelines.
